# E-identification (e-legitimation)

## What it is
An e-identification lets you prove your identity online, for example when you log in to the e-services
of Skatteverket, Försäkringskassan, CSN or your region's healthcare services.

## Common options
The most widely used e-identification in Sweden is BankID, which is issued by banks. Freja eID is
another approved e-identification that can be ordered through an app. To get an e-identification you
normally need a Swedish personal identity number.

## Security advice
Never log in with your e-identification because someone called you and asked you to. Public agencies
and banks never ask you to identify yourself with BankID during a phone call that they initiated.
