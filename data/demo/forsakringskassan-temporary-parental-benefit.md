# Temporary parental benefit (tillfällig föräldrapenning), "VAB"

## When it applies
You can receive temporary parental benefit when you stay home from work to care for a sick child who
is under 12 years old. Staying home to care for a sick child is often called "vabba".

## How many days
Temporary parental benefit can be paid for up to 120 days per child and year.

## Reporting and applying
Tell your employer that you are staying home, and report the absence (VAB) to Försäkringskassan.
You must apply for the benefit no later than 90 days after the first day you stayed home.
