# Applying for an identity card (id-kort) from Skatteverket

## Who can apply
Skatteverket issues identity cards to people who are registered in the Swedish population register
(folkbokförda) and have a personal identity number.

## How to apply
1. Pay the application fee before your visit.
2. Visit one of Skatteverket's service offices in person. You are photographed at the office.
3. Prove your identity with a valid identity document. If you do not have one, a person who knows you
   and holds a valid Swedish identity document can vouch for your identity (intygsgivare).

## Collecting the card
The card is collected in person at the service office you choose when you apply.
