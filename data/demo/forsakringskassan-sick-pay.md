# Sick pay (sjuklön) and sickness benefit (sjukpenning) for employees

## The first 14 days
If you are employed and become ill, you must report sick to your employer. The employer pays sick pay
(sjuklön) for the first 14 days of the sick period. A deduction (karensavdrag) is made from the sick pay.

## Medical certificate
From the eighth day of the sick period you need a medical certificate (läkarintyg) that shows how the
illness reduces your ability to work.

## From day 15
If you are still ill after 14 days, the employer reports this to Försäkringskassan, and you can apply
for sickness benefit (sjukpenning) from Försäkringskassan from day 15.
