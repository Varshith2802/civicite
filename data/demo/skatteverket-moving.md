# Reporting a move (flyttanmälan)

## Who must report
Everyone who is registered in the Swedish population register (folkbokföring) and moves to a new
permanent address in Sweden must report the move to Skatteverket. Your registered address affects,
among other things, which municipality and region you belong to, where you pay municipal tax, and
where you receive healthcare and school placements.

## Deadline
You must report the move to Skatteverket within one week after you have moved.

## How to report
The easiest way is the e-service for reporting a move on Skatteverket's website, where you log in
with an e-identification such as BankID or Freja eID. You can also report on a paper form
(flyttanmälan) and send it by post. Children who move with their guardians are included when the
guardians report the move.

## Mail forwarding is a separate service
Reporting a move to Skatteverket updates your registered address. Forwarding of letters and parcels
(eftersändning) is a separate paid service that is not handled by Skatteverket.
