# Personal identity number (personnummer) and coordination number (samordningsnummer)

## Personal identity number
A personal identity number (personnummer) is assigned by Skatteverket to everyone who is registered
in the Swedish population register (folkbokförd). Children born in Sweden get one at birth. A person
who moves to Sweden is normally registered if they are expected to live in Sweden for at least one year.

## Format
The number has ten digits: the date of birth as YYMMDD, followed by a hyphen and four digits
(YYMMDD-XXXX). The last digit is a check digit calculated with the Luhn algorithm. When a person turns
100 years old, the hyphen is replaced by a plus sign. In many digital systems the twelve-digit form
YYYYMMDD-XXXX is used.

## Coordination number
A coordination number (samordningsnummer) is an identity number for people who are not, or have not
been, registered in the population register, for example people who work in Sweden for a short period.
It has the same structure as a personal identity number, but 60 is added to the day of birth, so a
person born on the 5th of a month gets 65 as the day digits.

## Protecting the number
A personal identity number is not secret in Sweden, but you should be careful about sharing it
together with other personal data, because it can be misused for identity fraud.
