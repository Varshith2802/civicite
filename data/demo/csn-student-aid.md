# Student aid (studiemedel)

## What it consists of
Student aid (studiemedel) from CSN consists of a grant (bidrag), which you do not pay back, and a
loan (lån), which you repay. You can choose to take only the grant.

## Requirements
You must be studying at least half-time (50 percent) in a programme that qualifies for student aid.
To keep receiving student aid, you need to pass your studies at a sufficient pace; CSN checks your
study results before granting aid for a new period.

## Applying
You apply to CSN for each study period, normally in CSN's e-service with an e-identification. It is
a good idea to apply well before the period starts so that the money is paid on time.
