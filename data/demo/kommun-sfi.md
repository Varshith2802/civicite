# Swedish for immigrants (SFI)

## What SFI is
Swedish for immigrants (svenska för invandrare, SFI) is a free basic Swedish language course for adults
whose first language is not Swedish. SFI is organised by the municipalities.

## Who can study
To study SFI you generally need to be registered (folkbokförd) in the municipality and lack basic
knowledge of Swedish.

## How to apply
You apply to the adult education unit (vuxenutbildning) of the municipality where you are registered,
usually through the municipality's website.
