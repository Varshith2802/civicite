# Parental benefit (föräldrapenning)

## How many days
Parents can receive parental benefit for a total of 480 days per child. When the parents have joint
custody, the days are divided equally between them, and 90 days are reserved for each parent. Reserved
days cannot be transferred to the other parent.

## Benefit levels
Most of the days are paid at sickness benefit level, which is based on your income. The remaining days
are paid at a fixed minimum level.

## Saving days for later
Parental benefit can be used until the child turns 12 or finishes the fifth year of school. After the
child turns four, a maximum of 96 days can be saved for later use.

## How to apply
You apply to Försäkringskassan, normally through its e-service with an e-identification. You should
also tell your employer about planned parental leave in good time.
