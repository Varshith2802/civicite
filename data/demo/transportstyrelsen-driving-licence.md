# Using a foreign driving licence in Sweden

## Licences from the EU or EEA
A valid driving licence issued in an EU or EEA country is valid in Sweden. You do not need to exchange it
for a Swedish licence while it is valid.

## Licences from other countries
A valid licence from a country outside the EU/EEA can be used in Sweden for a limited time. Once you are
registered in the Swedish population register, it is valid for one year from the date of registration.
After that you need a Swedish driving licence. Licences from some countries can be exchanged for a
Swedish licence; for others you must take the Swedish driving test.

## Applying
Applications to exchange a licence are made to Transportstyrelsen.
