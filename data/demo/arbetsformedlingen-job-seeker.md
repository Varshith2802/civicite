# Registering as a job seeker (inskrivning) and unemployment insurance (a-kassa)

## Register on your first day of unemployment
If you become unemployed, register as a job seeker with Arbetsförmedlingen on your first day of
unemployment. Registration is required to receive unemployment benefit.

## Unemployment insurance fund
Unemployment benefit is paid by an unemployment insurance fund (a-kassa), not by Arbetsförmedlingen.
You apply for the benefit to your a-kassa after registering with Arbetsförmedlingen.

## Activity reports
While registered, you report your job search activities to Arbetsförmedlingen every month in an
activity report (aktivitetsrapport).
